Support

Questions, bugs or a lost purchase? Write to neonbreakergame@gmail.com.
Please include your Player ID — you find it at the bottom of Settings in the game.

Frequently asked questions

I bought "No Ads" and changed my phone. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases (same Apple ID).

My progress is gone after reinstalling. Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

How do I turn off notifications? iPhone Settings → Notifications → Neon Breaker.

Refunds for purchases made through the App Store are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
