Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Neon Breaker ("the game", "we") handles information. The game is developed by Dimitry Kofman (individual developer, Israel). Contact: neonbreakergame@gmail.com.

1. No account, no personal data from us

The game has no registration or login. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts. Your progress (levels, coins, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the game. The game creates a random Player ID, shown in Settings, only so you can quote it in a support request.

2. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store). We never receive your payment details. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase happened, so the item can be delivered and restored.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional reward videos provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA — only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and OS version, and interactions with ads. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Tracking permission. On iPhone, the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) before personalised ads are used. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will see non-personalised ads and keep all game rewards. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

EEA, UK and Switzerland. Before ads are shown, a consent form (Google UMP) lets you accept or decline personalised advertising. You can change your choice later in the game settings.

If you buy "No Ads", no ads are shown.

4. Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example when your lives are full). They are created by the game itself; no server and no personal data are involved. You can turn them off in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Your rights

Because we do not store personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to export or delete; deleting the game removes all its data from your device. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the links above or contact us and we will help.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
